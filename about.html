About Aklavya Kumar

Working at the intersection of Computer Science, Neurotechnology, Physics, and Autonomous Robotics in Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania.

AKLAVYA KUMAR // MECHANICSBURG, PA
CUMBERLAND VALLEY HIGH SCHOOL // CLASS OF 2027

Engineering Hardware, Neural Systems, and Software

I am Aklavya Kumar, a senior at Cumberland Valley High School in Mechanicsburg, PA. My work spans autonomous control systems, biomedical signal processing, and competitive algorithms.

In neurotechnology, I engineered CogniTrack, an EEG-based Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) platform in Python and PyTorch trained on more than 500 neural signal datasets to measure cognitive fatigue, and wrote the book "From Healing to Superhuman: How Brain-Computer Interfaces Restore Broken Bodies and Enhance Daily Life", available on Amazon.

In robotics and engineering, I serve as Build & Programming Lead for FIRST Tech Challenge Team 8468 The Javengers (FIRST Dean's List Semi-Finalist) and lead robotics developer for CVHS Science Olympiad (30+ invitational medals and consecutive Top-20 National team finishes).
